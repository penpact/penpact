# Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Engineering draft; have a licensed attorney review before relying
> on it. Entity name and jurisdiction are placeholders. This DPA forms part of the
> [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

**Last updated:** 2026-06-03

## 1. Roles

For personal data contained in Customer Data, **Customer is the controller** and **Penpact is the
processor** (or sub-processor where Customer is itself a processor). Penpact processes such data only
on Customer's documented instructions, including via use of the Service and these Terms.

## 2. Subject matter & duration

Penpact processes personal data to provide the e-signature Service for the duration of the agreement
and any wind-down/retention period.

## 3. Nature & purpose; categories

- **Purpose:** create, send, and capture electronic signatures and the legally-required audit trail.
- **Data subjects:** Customer's senders and signers.
- **Categories:** names, email addresses, signature values, and signing evidence (timestamps, IP,
  user agent, approximate geolocation, consent records, device hints), plus document contents that
  Customer chooses to upload.

## 4. Customer obligations

Customer warrants it has a lawful basis to provide the data and to instruct the described processing,
and that it has obtained any necessary notices/consents from its signers.

## 5. Penpact obligations

Penpact will: (a) process only on documented instructions; (b) ensure personnel are bound by
confidentiality; (c) implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures (Art. 32
GDPR) — encryption in transit, access control, hashed API keys, append-only audit logs, object-lock
document retention; (d) assist Customer with data-subject requests and with security/DPIA obligations
to the extent applicable; (e) notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal
data breach.

## 6. Sub-processors

Customer authorizes Penpact to engage sub-processors to provide the Service (cloud hosting, managed
Postgres, object storage, email delivery, and — when AI field-detection is used — Anthropic for
document processing). Penpact will maintain a current list, impose data-protection terms no less
protective than this DPA, and give notice of changes with a right to object.

## 7. International transfers

Where transfers occur, the parties rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. EU Standard Contractual
Clauses), incorporated by reference.

## 8. Deletion & return

On termination, Penpact will, at Customer's choice and within a reasonable period, return or delete
personal data, subject to legal retention requirements and routine backup cycles.

## 9. Audits

Penpact will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance and, subject
to confidentiality and reasonable limits, allow for audits.
